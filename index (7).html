What Retype speaks

Each format converts into whichever of the others makes sense — text-based formats keep their structure where possible.

.PDF

Extracts the text layer of a PDF and retypes it as .txt, .md, or a fresh .docx.

.DOCX

Reads a Word document's content and structure, and outputs .txt, .md, .html, or .pdf.

.MD

Renders Markdown into .html or .pdf, or flattens it down to .txt / .docx.

.HTML

Converts marked-up pages into .md, .txt, .pdf, or .docx.

.TXT

Wraps plain text into a formatted .pdf, .docx, .md, or .html document.
